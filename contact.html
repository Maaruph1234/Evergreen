Frequently Asked Questions

How can I donate to Evergreen Lifecare?
You can donate directly through our Donate page. We accept bank transfers and online payments. Every naira goes directly to our programs.
How do I apply for the tech training bootcamp?
Fill out our application form at evergreenlifecare.org/apply.html — it takes about 3 minutes. Applications are free and open to Nigerian youth aged 16–35.
Can organisations partner with Evergreen Lifecare?
Absolutely. We welcome corporate sponsors, NGO partners, government agencies, and community organisations. Use the contact form to start the conversation.
Do you operate outside Kebbi State?
We are currently based in Birnin Kebbi, Kebbi State, but our vision is national. We are actively planning expansion to other states in 2026.
How can I volunteer remotely?
We have remote roles in content creation, research, M&E, and online mentorship. Visit our Volunteer page and select "Remote / Online" as your availability.

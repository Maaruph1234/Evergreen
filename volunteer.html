Give Your Time.
Change a Life.

Evergreen Lifecare is powered by people who choose to show up. Whether you have one hour or one month, your contribution matters deeply to the communities we serve.

Why Volunteer

You Have Something Nigeria Needs

Whether you are a doctor, designer, developer, teacher, or simply someone with a willing heart — there is a place for you at Evergreen Lifecare.

Build Real-World Experience

Work on meaningful projects alongside passionate professionals. Add genuine impact work to your portfolio and CV.

Expand Your Network

Connect with like-minded Nigerians, NGO leaders, tech professionals, and community changemakers.

Earn a Certificate of Service

All volunteers receive an official Evergreen Lifecare certificate recognising their contribution and hours served.

Open Roles

Find Your Role

We need people across many disciplines. Find the role that fits your skills and passion.

Tech Trainer / Facilitator

Teach web development, UI/UX, digital marketing, or cybersecurity during our bootcamp programs. Perfect for developers, designers, and digital marketers.

10 Days per Cohort

Health Worker / Medical Professional

Provide free consultations, screenings, or health education during our community healthcare outreach days. Open to doctors, nurses, pharmacists, and health educators.

Flexible — 1 to 3 Days

Content Creator / Photographer

Capture and document our programs, create social media content, and help tell the Evergreen story to the world through compelling visuals and writing.

Remote or On-Site

Education Mentor / Tutor

Provide academic mentorship, tutoring, or career guidance to students and youth in our education support program. All subjects and levels welcome.

Flexible Schedule

Outreach & Mobilisation

Help us reach communities, recruit beneficiaries, distribute materials, and coordinate on-the-ground activities during events and program launches.

Events-Based

Admin, M&E & Research

Support our monitoring and evaluation, data collection, reporting, grant writing, and administrative functions. Great for graduates and young professionals.

Remote Friendly
How It Works

4 Simple Steps to Join

Getting involved with Evergreen Lifecare takes less than 5 minutes to start.

01

Fill the Application

Complete our short volunteer form below — it takes under 3 minutes and tells us about your skills and availability.

02

We Review & Match

Our team reviews your application and matches you to the most suitable role based on your skills and schedule.

03

Orientation & Onboarding

You'll receive a welcome message, program brief, and orientation details before your first assignment.

04

Show Up & Make Impact

Arrive, serve, and help transform lives. A certificate of service will be issued after your commitment is complete.

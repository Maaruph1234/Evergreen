How Technology Can Change Your Life Starting From Zero

May 10, 2026 Aminu Kabir Shamaki 5 min read

In 2026, a young man from Birnin Kebbi with no laptop, no prior tech experience, and no stable income walked into our first bootcamp. Ten days later, he had built his first website, landed a freelance client, and earned more in one week than he had in the previous three months. This is not an exception — it is what happens when you give Nigerian youth the right tools and the right environment.

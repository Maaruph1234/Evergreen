Why We Exist

Millions of people continue to face barriers to healthcare, education, livelihoods, social protection, and humanitarian support. We exist to bridge these gaps by delivering innovative, community-driven, and sustainable solutions that leave no one behind.

Our Mission

A Humanitarian Organization Built on Purpose

Evergreen Lifecare Support Foundation (ELSF) is a registered non-profit humanitarian and development organization committed to improving the health, wellbeing, and socio-economic resilience of vulnerable individuals and communities across Nigeria.

  • Integrated health & nutrition programming (RMNCAH+N), disease prevention, and emergency preparedness
  • Women, youth & livelihoods empowerment through education, skills training, and economic opportunities
  • Humanitarian response, peacebuilding, social inclusion & community resilience programs

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Every decision, every intervention, and every partnership is rooted in these five guiding principles.

Integrity & Accountability
We uphold the highest standards of transparency, ethical conduct, and responsible stewardship while remaining accountable to communities, partners, and donors.
Compassion & Human Dignity
We serve with empathy, respect, and a commitment to protecting the rights, wellbeing, and dignity of every individual.
Equity, Inclusion & Social Justice
We champion equal opportunities and ensure that vulnerable and marginalized populations are not left behind.
Excellence & Innovation
We pursue quality, professionalism, continuous learning, and innovative solutions that create lasting impact.
Collaboration & Sustainability
We believe transformative and sustainable change is achieved through partnerships, community ownership, resilience building, and collective action.
Our Vision
A world where every individual and community thrives in health, dignity, peace, resilience, and prosperity.

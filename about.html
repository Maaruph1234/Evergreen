About Us

Who We Are

Evergreen Lifecare Support Foundation (ELSF) is a registered non-profit, non-political humanitarian and development organization committed to improving the health, wellbeing, and socio-economic resilience of vulnerable individuals and communities.

We support women, children, youth, persons with disabilities, widows, the elderly, and underserved populations by expanding access to quality services, economic opportunities, social protection, and sustainable development initiatives.

A key pillar of our work is Integrated Health and Nutrition Programming, with particular emphasis on Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child and Adolescent Health and Nutrition (RMNCAH+N), immunization, disease prevention, and primary healthcare strengthening.

Our Vision

A world where every individual and community thrives in health, dignity, peace, resilience, and prosperity.

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable populations and strengthen communities through integrated health, humanitarian, education, social protection, peacebuilding, and economic empowerment interventions that create sustainable and inclusive development outcomes.

Our Work

Our Flagship Programs

Six integrated pillars guide our work — each chosen because of the deep, urgent needs we see across communities in Nigeria.

Health & Nutrition (RMNCAH+N)

Objective

Improving access to quality, equitable, and integrated health and nutrition services through strengthened health systems, community engagement, disease prevention, immunization, and emergency preparedness.

Improved health & nutrition outcomes for vulnerable populations

Humanitarian Response & Resilience

Objective

Providing timely humanitarian assistance to communities affected by disasters, conflicts, disease outbreaks, displacement, and other emergencies while strengthening long-term resilience.

Reduced vulnerability & increased community resilience

Women & Youth Empowerment

Objective

Empowering women, girls, and young people through leadership development, education, entrepreneurship, vocational skills, economic opportunities, and social participation.

Empowered women & youth contributing to sustainable development

Education, Skills & Livelihoods

Objective

Supporting access to quality education, lifelong learning, vocational training, entrepreneurship development, financial inclusion, and livelihood opportunities for individuals and households.

Improved educational attainment & economic wellbeing

Social Inclusion & Community Development

Objective

Promoting the wellbeing, protection, and inclusion of vulnerable populations while strengthening community systems and local development structures for resilient, self-reliant communities.

Stronger, more inclusive & resilient communities

Peacebuilding, Advocacy & Research

Objective

Promoting peaceful coexistence, evidence generation, policy engagement, community dialogue, and social cohesion to drive sustainable development and evidence-informed decision making.

Enhanced social cohesion & sustainable policy impact
